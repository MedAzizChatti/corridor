# Milestone 1 — Definition of done

- **Agreed:** 2026-11-20
- **Status:** Closed list. Anything new goes into Milestone 2.
- **Goal:** learning, and a working, proven, showable project for the portfolio. Not real users.

## It works end to end
- [ ] **US East Coast world** (main demo): the simulator runs, and trucks move on real roads.
- [ ] **Spain world (Valencia region):** a flash-flood scenario replaying the real October 2024 DANA weather data.
- [ ] Hazard polygons derived from Open-Meteo data, shown on the map.
- [ ] **S1 (ask):** "Which deliveries are affected?" shows a map plus a table of affected trucks and orders. EN and FR.
- [ ] **S2 (act):** "Reroute them to the Philadelphia warehouse" creates a Transfer Order; a **manager** approves it; it executes exactly once; the map updates; `approved_by` is recorded.
- [ ] **S3 (stale):** approving after the truck passed the exit gives `stale`, and the agent re-plans.
- [ ] **S4 (chart):** "On-time rate by warehouse this week" shows a chart.
- [ ] **S5 (Gantt):** "How does the storm delay today's deliveries?" shows a Gantt of planned vs. revised times.
- [ ] **S6 (permissions):** a **dispatcher** asks the agent to approve, and is refused by the tool (not the prompt).
- [ ] Auth: JWT, `dispatcher` and `manager` roles, and a demo-login button.

## It's proven
- [ ] Unit and harness tests (ScriptedModel) green in CI on every push.
- [ ] Concurrency tests (exactly-once, capacity, stale) against real Postgres in CI.
- [ ] At least 25 eval cases, including at least 4 in the Spain world and a confused-deputy trap.
- [ ] **pass^3 ≥ 80%** on the chosen free model, and **0 unapproved writes**.

## It's showable
- [ ] README: architecture diagram, eval table, latency and token numbers, honest limitations.
- [ ] A 2-minute demo video covering S1–S6.
- [ ] ADRs up to date, plus a journal with real failure stories and numbers.

## Not in M1 (candidates for M2)
Live deployment · Tunisia world · cross-border world · LangGraph comparison and posts · Arabic and Darja · non-weather disruptions (road closures, strikes, ports).
